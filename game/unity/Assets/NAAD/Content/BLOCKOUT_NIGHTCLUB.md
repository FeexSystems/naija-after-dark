# Nightclub Visual Blockout

## Overview

The Nightclub is a primary destination in the Island Nightlife district. This blockout focuses on the entrance area (queue management, security checkpoint) and the main interior (stage, dance floor, bar, VIP areas). The design emphasizes atmosphere, lighting, and social interaction.

## Location Context

### District Placement
- **Primary**: Island Nightlife (main implementation)
- **Secondary**: Creative District (underground venue variant)

### Building Integration
- **Placement**: Standalone building or ground floor of high-rise
- **Size**: 20m x 30m footprint (large venue)
- **Capacity**: 200-300 people
- **Access**: Street entrance, VIP entrance (optional)

- **Lighting**: LED strips or neon signage

#### Queue Management
- **Velvet Rope**: Red or black velvet
- **Stanchions**: Chrome or brass posts
- **Height**: 1.0 meter
- **Spacing**: 1.5 meters between posts
- **Layout**: Zigzag or straight line

#### Bouncer Stand
- **Type**: Raised platform or designated area
- **Height**: 0.2 meters (raised platform)
- **Size**: 2m x 1m
- **Material**: Metal or wood

- **Type**: Double glass doors
- **Height**: 2.5 meters

## Technical Specifications

### Collision Meshes
- **Walls**: Full collision
- **Floors**: Walkable surfaces
- **Stage**: Walkable (with collision for edge)
- **Bar Counter**: Collision
- **Furniture**: Collision where appropriate
- **Doors**: Collision (unless openable)

### Navigation
- **NavMesh**: Bake for entire interior
- **Agent Height**: 2.0 meters
- **Agent Radius**: 0.5 meters
- **Step Height**: 0.4 meters
- **Slope Limit**: 45 degrees
- **Off-Mesh Links**: For stage access

### Lighting
- **Baked**: Static structure lighting
- **Realtime**: Dynamic lighting effects
- **Light Probes**: For character lighting
- **Reflection Probes**: For reflective surfaces
- **Emissive**: Neon signs, LED displays

### Effects
- **Fog**: For atmosphere
- **Bloom**: Post-processing for neon
- **Particles**: Confetti, fog machine
- **Sound**: Music, crowd noise

## Performance Considerations

### Optimization
- **LODs**: For distant elements
- **Occlusion**: For interior spaces
- **Light Baking**: For static elements
- **Realtime Lights**: Limit count
- **Particle Limits**: Control particle count

### Mobile Optimization
- **Reduced Light Count**: Fewer realtime lights
- **Simplified Effects**: Reduce bloom, particles
- **Light Probes**: Instead of realtime lights
- **Occlusion**: Critical for performance
- **Material Simplification**: Reduce shader complexity

- **Dress Code**: Often stylish, fashionable
- **Social**: Important for networking and dating
- **Timing**: Peak hours: 10 PM - 3 AM
- **VIP Culture**: Status symbol, premium service
- **Security**: Bouncers, ID checks common

### Visual Authenticity
- **Crowd**: Busy, energetic atmosphere
- **Lighting**: Dynamic, colorful, changing
- **Fashion**: Stylish outfits visible
- **Social**: Groups, couples, singles
- **Energy**: High energy, movement

### Cultural Elements
- **Music**: Nigerian and international hits
- **Drinks**: Local and imported brands
- **Social Media**: Photo opportunities important
- **Networking**: Business and social connections
