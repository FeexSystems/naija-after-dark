# NAAD District Variants

## Overview

The NAAD megacity is divided into four distinct district variants, each with unique visual characteristics, atmosphere, and gameplay focus. These districts are connected to form a cohesive, believable environment.

## 1. Island Nightlife District

### Inspiration
Victoria Island, Lagos - affluent nightlife destination

### Visual Characteristics
- **Architecture**: Modern high-rise buildings, glass facades, sleek designs
- **Lighting**: Abundant neon signage, LED displays, colorful ambient lighting
- **Materials**: Polished concrete, glass, metal, premium finishes
- **Colors**: Cool blues, purples, pinks (neon palette)
- **Density**: Medium density with wide streets and open spaces

### Key Locations
- **Nightclubs**: Multiple high-end clubs with elaborate entrances
- **Bars and Lounges**: Rooftop bars, cocktail lounges
- **Restaurants**: Fine dining establishments
- **Hotels**: Luxury hotels with lobbies and terraces
- **Waterfront**: Marina views, waterfront promenade

### Asset Variants
- **Buildings**: `APT_Building_High`, `APT_Building_Medium` with glass facades
- **Lighting**: `NCL_Signage_Neon`, `NCL_Signage_LED`, `STR_Streetlight_Decorative`
- **Roads**: `RD_Surface_Asphalt` with high-quality markings
- **Utilities**: `UTL_AC_Standing`, `UTL_Cable_Bundle` (tidy, concealed)
- **Signage**: Premium, illuminated signage

### Atmosphere
- **Time of Day**: Primarily night, sunset transitions
- **Mood**: Energetic, upscale, vibrant
- **Audio**: Bass-heavy music, crowd chatter, distant traffic
- **Weather**: Clear nights, occasional light rain (wet street reflections)

### Gameplay Focus
- Social interactions
- Nightlife activities (dancing, drinking, socializing)
- Player progression through club hierarchy
- High-stakes social encounters

## 2. Mainland Streets District

### Inspiration
Ikeja, Surulere - dense urban residential and commercial areas

### Visual Characteristics
- **Architecture**: Mixed low-rise and medium-rise buildings, colorful shopfronts
- **Lighting**: Functional street lighting, shop illumination, warm tones
- **Materials**: Painted concrete, brick, corrugated metal, wood
- **Colors**: Warm yellows, oranges, reds, earth tones
- **Density**: High density, narrow streets, crowded sidewalks

### Key Locations
- **Residential Areas**: Apartment blocks, family homes
- **Commercial Streets**: Shopfronts, markets, kiosks
- **Suya Spots**: Street food vendors
- **Transportation**: Bus stops, taxi stands
- **Community Spaces**: Small parks, gathering spots

### Asset Variants
- **Buildings**: `APT_Building_Low`, `APT_Building_Medium` with painted facades
- **Shopfronts**: `SHP_Front_Mixed`, `SHP_Front_Rolling`, `SHP_Awning`
- **Kiosks**: `KSK_Structure_Standard`, `KSK_Counter`
- **Roads**: `RD_Surface_Asphalt` with wear and tear
- **Utilities**: `UTL_Generator_Portable`, `UTL_Pole_Concrete`, visible cables
- **Signage**: `SHP_Signage_Banner`, `SHP_Signage_Box` (varied quality)

### Atmosphere
- **Time of Day**: All times, vibrant during day and night
- **Mood**: Bustling, authentic, lived-in
- **Audio**: Street vendors, traffic, music from shops, conversations
- **Weather**: Humid heat, afternoon thunderstorms, wet streets

### Gameplay Focus
- Everyday interactions
- Street food (suya spots)
- Shopping and commerce
- Community events
- Navigation through crowded spaces

## 3. Creative District

### Inspiration
Lekki Phase 1, Yaba - emerging tech and creative hub

### Visual Characteristics
- **Architecture**: Converted industrial buildings, modern mixed-use, artistic facades
- **Lighting**: Industrial fixtures, artistic lighting, warm white LEDs
- **Materials**: Exposed brick, concrete, metal, glass, street art
- **Colors**: Muted grays with vibrant accent colors (street art)
- **Density**: Medium-low density, open spaces, large buildings

### Key Locations
- **Art Galleries**: Converted warehouse spaces
- **Tech Hubs**: Co-working spaces, startup offices
- **Cafes**: Trendy coffee shops with outdoor seating
- **Street Art**: Murals, graffiti, installations
- **Event Spaces**: Pop-up venues, exhibition halls

### Asset Variants
- **Buildings**: `APT_Building_Medium` (industrial conversion style)
- **Shopfronts**: `SHP_Front_Glass` (large, open)
- **Lighting**: `STR_Streetlight_Standard` (industrial style)
- **Roads**: `RD_Surface_Paved` (brick or decorative concrete)
- **Utilities**: `UTL_AC_Wall`, visible industrial elements
- **Signage**: Minimalist, artistic signage

### Atmosphere
- **Time of Day**: Day and evening
- **Mood**: Creative, modern, inspiring
- **Audio**: Ambient music, conversations, keyboard sounds, distant construction
- **Weather**: Bright days, golden hour sunsets

### Gameplay Focus
- Creative interactions
- Tech and business encounters
- Art and culture events
- Networking opportunities
- Exploration and discovery

## 4. Coastal District

### Inspiration
Lekki Beach, Elegushi Beach - coastal leisure destination

### Visual Characteristics
- **Architecture**: Low-rise structures, open designs, tropical aesthetics
- **Lighting**: Natural sunlight, warm evening lighting, tiki torches
- **Materials**: Wood, thatch, canvas, stone, sand
- **Colors**: Warm beiges, tropical greens, ocean blues, sunset oranges
- **Density**: Low density, open spaces, natural elements

### Key Locations
- **Beach Resort**: Main resort complex with cabanas
- **Beachfront**: Sandy beach, shoreline, water
- **Jetty/Pier**: Wooden pier extending into water
- **Beach Bars**: Open-air bars and restaurants
- **Water Sports**: Equipment rental, activity areas

### Asset Variants
- **Structures**: `BCH_Cabana_Structure`, `BCH_Cabana_Fabric`, `BCH_Jetty_Wood`
- **Furniture**: `BCH_Lounger_Chair`, `BCH_Lounger_Umbrella`
- **Natural**: `BCH_Palm_Tree`, `BCH_Palm_Sapling`
- **Surfaces**: `BCH_Sand_Surface`, `BCH_Water_Surface`, `BCH_Shoreline_Wet`
- **Lighting**: `SYU_Lantern_String`, natural lighting
- **Signage**: Minimal, tropical-themed

### Atmosphere
- **Time of Day**: Day (beach activities), sunset (romantic), night (beach parties)
- **Mood**: Relaxed, tropical, leisure
- **Audio**: Ocean waves, seagulls, beach music, distant traffic
- **Weather**: Sunny days, humid heat, sea breeze, occasional rain showers

### Gameplay Focus
- Leisure activities
- Beach parties and events
- Water sports
- Romantic encounters
- Relaxation and downtime

## District Connectivity

### Transition Zones
Each district connects to others through transition zones that blend visual characteristics:

**Island ↔ Mainland**
- Bridge crossing with mixed architecture
- Gradual shift from modern to traditional
- Lighting transition from neon to warm streetlights

**Mainland ↔ Creative**
- Redevelopment zone showing old and new
- Street art appearing more frequently
- Building styles mixing industrial and residential

**Creative ↔ Coastal**
- Coastal road with modern amenities
- Palm trees appearing alongside modern buildings
- Lighting shifting from industrial to warm/tropical

**Coastal ↔ Island**
- Waterfront promenade connecting beach to marina
- Luxury condos transitioning to resort
- Lighting blending warm beach tones with cool neon

### Navigation
- Clear visual landmarks in each district
- Distinct color palettes for orientation
- Signage indicating district boundaries
- Pathways guide player between key locations

## Asset Sharing Between Districts

### Shared Core Assets
- Basic road and sidewalk modules
- Standard utility poles and cables
- Common furniture (chairs, tables)
- Base building materials

### District-Specific Variants
- **Island**: Premium versions with higher detail
- **Mainland**: Weathered, lived-in versions
- **Creative**: Industrial/artistic variations
- **Coastal**: Tropical, weather-resistant versions

### Material Variants
- Same geometry, different materials per district
- Color palette variations
- Weathering and wear levels
- Lighting response differences

## Performance Considerations

### Island Nightlife
- High lighting complexity (neon, LEDs)
- Requires careful light baking
- Consider LOD for distant buildings
- Optimize emissive materials

### Mainland Streets
- High object density
- Requires instanced rendering
- Occlusion culling essential
- Texture atlasing for small props

### Creative District
- Large open spaces
- Good for occlusion culling
- Moderate lighting complexity
- Balance detail vs performance

### Coastal
- Open environment with long sightlines
- Water rendering optimization
- Foliage LOD critical
- Dynamic lighting for day/night cycle
